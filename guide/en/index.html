RayTok User Guide

v1 · Android · App version 1.0.0 · 2026-09-15

RayTok is a real-time translation app designed for use with Bluetooth earbuds and glasses. When a pair of earbuds is shared, the other person's language is heard in the left ear and your language in the right ear — two people can hold a conversation with just one phone. When language packs are downloaded, speech recognition, translation, and audio run on-device, allowing you to use it without an internet connection.

1. Preparation

Installation

  • Search for "RayTok" on the Play Store, or visit raytok.kr/download
  • Android 7.0 or higher

Connecting earbuds or glasses

  1. Phone Settings → Bluetooth → Connect earbuds (or glasses)
  2. On Samsung phones, turn off Dolby Atmos — if it is on, left and right audio will not be separated. Settings → Sounds and vibration → Sound quality and effects → Dolby Atmos off. Also turn off "Mono audio" in Accessibility.

First run — First-time setup 1 / 2

  1. Choose languages to use — Select your language and the other person's language, and the language packs (translation and recognition) will be downloaded immediately. Korean and English are downloaded automatically. If there is no internet connection, you can download them later in Settings.
  2. Next: check left / right → Check the earbud sides — Wear both earbuds and press. "Left" must come only from the left, "Right" only from the right. If heard from both sides, turn off Dolby Atmos and Mono audio (Open sound settings) and check again.
  3. Start. You can reopen this screen anytime from Settings → System → Show first-time setup again.

2. Home screen

Home — Five cards and app language indicator

  • App language: Tap the language indicator at the top to change the display language (default: Korean).
  • My language: Changing it in one place (your box on the Conversation screen, or in Settings) updates Conversation, Lecture, and Notes together.
  • Menu cards: Conversation / Lecture / Two-phone conversation / Notes / Settings. The bottom tabs are identical. Language packs are located in Settings → Languages (Chapter 7).

3. Conversation — Two people with one phone

Conversation — Top half is for the other person (upside down), bottom half is for you

When talking face-to-face. Share a pair of earbuds (one earbud each) and place the phone between you.

  1. When you enter Conversation, the top box is for the other person and the bottom box is for your language. Press and hold a box to select a language.
  2. Tap Start and simply talk. When finished, tap Stop.
  3. The app automatically determines who is speaking. If recognized incorrectly, tap the turn box once to set "I am speaking now / the other person is speaking now".
Audio routing (Automatic, no selection needed)
  • When the other person speaks → Translation plays in the right earbud (the side you wear)
  • When you speak → Translation plays in the left earbud (the side the other person wears)
  • The phone speaker remains quiet. If no earbuds are connected, sound plays through the phone speaker.
  • Screen: Top is the other person (upside down for easy reading), bottom is you. Translations appear large, original text small.
  • If heard in both ears, tapping the line at the very top of the screen — "Hearing it in both ears? → tap to turn off Atmos" — opens sound settings.
  • From More in the top right, select Keep this conversation — the conversation so far is saved to Notes.

4. Lecture — When one person speaks continuously

Lecture — Original text builds up above and translation below, sentence by sentence

When one person speaks at length, such as in lectures, announcements, or meetings. Translates continuously until you tap Stop.

  1. Enter Lecture and select the Speaker's language from the language indicator in the top right (it must differ from your language for translation to occur).
  2. Start with the button at the bottom center → original text (small) above, translation (bold) below flows on the screen.
  3. Press the ▶ button to have the translation read aloud. Press again to pause, and press once more to resume reading from where it stopped. If it falls behind, it automatically speeds up slightly.
  4. When finished, tap Stop → Save (disk icon) or Delete (trash can).
  • Each time a sentence finishes, the entire sentence is re-translated — natural without stitching fragments together.
  • When a USB microphone is plugged in, the Mic: auto / USB mic selection appears next to the language indicator. You can place the microphone close to the speaker.
  • Saved content can be reviewed and read aloud again in Notes.

5. Two-phone conversation — Two phones, each with earbuds

Two-phone — One person shows the QR code, the other scans it

Each person uses their own phone and earbuds to talk, whether sitting face-to-face or apart. No internet is needed. Both phones only need to be on the same Wi-Fi, or one phone turns on a hotspot and the other joins it.

  1. One person goes to Two-phone conversation → Create a connection. A QR code, address, and check code appear on the screen.
  2. The other person goes to Two-phone conversation → Scan the other phone's QR. If unable to scan the QR, enter the address and 6-digit check code in Type it in and tap Connect.
    If the other phone does not have the app, the QR code guides to the installation page (raytok.kr/download).
  3. When "Connected" appears, tap Start talking. Each person listens with their own earbuds and speaks into their own phone microphone. When finished, tap Disconnect.
If the prompt "This network has no internet access" appears → tap Keep (stay connected). The hotspot only links the phones together, so this is normal.

Even if the connection drops, your own record stays, and unsent lines will be sent when reconnected. Conversation content travels only between the two phones — a hotspot is safer than public Wi-Fi.

6. Notes — Voice notes

  • Notes → Voice note → Speak → The same button changes to Stop → It is saved.
  • Pausing for about 2 seconds will not cut off recording. Stopping does not make the content disappear.
  • Items saved from Lecture and Conversation also gather here. Tap a note to listen out loud with Read aloud.

7. Language packs and translation modes

Settings → Languages → Download languages

  • In Settings → Languages → Download languages, tap Download for the languages you want to use. Downloaded languages are translated without an internet connection. Downloading over Wi-Fi is recommended (around 30MB per language).
  • "ready" works without internet. "partial" means either translation or recognition uses the internet. Languages without a pack work only when connected to the internet.
  • Translation mode (same screen): Online for settled lines (default — only lines that stay on screen use the internet, while streaming intermediate results run on-device) / On-device first / Always online. Without an internet connection, every setting runs on-device.

8. License

  • Trial: A 7-day trial starts immediately upon installation.
  • Bundled device: Connecting RayTok-bundled earbuds or glasses once automatically authenticates this phone, after which it can be used even without the device.
  • When the trial ends, the screen locks and device guidance appears. Joining a two-phone conversation as a guest does not require a license — simply scan the other phone's QR code.

9. Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat to try
No sound from earbuds or glassesIf a call app was used just before, the device may remain in call mode. Turn Bluetooth off and on, or reconnect the device. If the device cannot receive playback, the app plays through the phone speaker.
Left and right not separated (Samsung)Turn off Dolby Atmos (Settings → Sounds and vibration → Sound quality and effects), and turn off Accessibility → Hearing enhancements → Mono audio. Tapping the top line of the Conversation screen opens this directly.
Recognition is slow or seeks internetIn Settings → Languages → Download languages, check if that language is "ready".
Other person's language is incorrectly recognizedTap the turn box (my side / their side) on the Conversation screen to set who is speaking. To change the language itself, press and hold the box.
Two-phone connection failsCheck whether both phones are on the same Wi-Fi, or one joined the other's hotspot. In the "No internet" prompt, select Keep. Many event venue public Wi-Fi networks block traffic between phones — use a hotspot. The first three numbers of "My address" on screen must match on both phones.
Want to report an issueSettings → Tap build number 5 times → Developer → Save recognizer log to notes. Sending us that note allows the cause to be found rather than guessed.

10. Current limitations

  • Phone call translation — Android does not provide phone call audio to third-party apps.
  • iPhone — v1 is Android-only.
  • Listening via earbud or glasses microphone — The phone microphone is used. USB microphones work in Lecture.

11. Privacy

  • RayTok has no servers. When language packs are downloaded, voice and translation are processed on-device.
  • When internet is connected, settled sentences may go to Google Translate depending on the translation mode setting, and speech recognition for languages without a pack goes to Google Speech Services. Nothing goes to the app operator.
  • Saved notes and lecture records remain only on the phone. For details, visit raytok.kr/privacy
