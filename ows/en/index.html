Interpreter earbuds
that are fine to hand over.

An open-ear design that hooks over the outer ear instead of going inside it. Hand one side to the other person, put the phone down, and talk face to face.

Why open-ear

With interpreter earbuds,
the other person has to wear one side

Earbuds that go inside the ear are hard to offer to someone you have just met. That is why we are making them in a form that does not block the ear.

Easy to hand over

It hooks on rather than going into the ear. Hand over one side and the conversation starts right away.

You hear your surroundings

You hear the other person's actual voice and the sounds around you as they are.

Cared for in the case

Because the earbuds are shared, we are preparing a charging case with a disinfecting function.

How to use it

One phone, one pair of earbuds, two people

1

Wear one side each

One side for the other person, one for you. The phone goes down on the table.

2

Just talk

The other person's words reach your ear in your language, and your words reach their ear in their language.

3

Even without internet

Download language packs in advance and you can use it on a plane or where roaming does not work.

Meeting venue — handing the right earbud to someone you have just met.

Café — take it, hook it on your ear and start talking right away.
