How to use it

The docent speaks,
and visitors look at the works

1

The docent, exactly as always

A small wireless microphone clipped on, phone in a pocket. Narration in Korean. Nothing about the tour changes.

2

Visitors scan one QR code

They scan the QR code at the information desk or the gallery entrance with their own phone and pick a language. No installing, no signing up.

3

Listen through earphones

They hear their own language through their own earphones, with their eyes on the works. Subtitles appear alongside.

The only new equipment in the gallery is one small wireless router. There is no procedure for handing out receivers and then collecting, charging, disinfecting and tracking lost ones.

Frequently asked questions

Check before you start

We already have an exhibition app. Isn't this the same thing?

No. An exhibition app is for a visitor listening alone to pre-recorded narration; RayTok carries a docent's live narration to a group in several languages. They are for different situations.

What has to be installed in the gallery?

One small wireless router. Nothing is attached to the objects or the walls. The QR code visitors scan can sit at the information desk or on the entrance signboard.

What file format should we send the narration script in?

Hangul (HWPX), Word (DOCX) and plain text all work. We send the translation back for the museum to review, edit and sign off. Planned for the November 2026 release.

What if a visitor has no earphones?

They can read the subtitles on screen. Some museums keep spare earphones at the information desk.

How many languages can run at once?

There is no structural limit on how many languages run at the same time. Each visitor receives the language they chose. Adding languages adds nothing to install in the gallery.

What happens to visitors' personal data?

Visitors do not sign up. No contact details are collected and no audio is stored.

Can we use it for a special exhibition only?

Yes. You can run it for the exhibition's dates and stop there. Running the permanent collection and special exhibitions separately is fine too.

Start with 30 days in one gallery

Pick one special exhibition or one gallery, use it for 30 days and decide from there. Email us your institution's name and how your docent tours run.
