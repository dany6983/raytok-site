How to use it

The professor just speaks.
I watch the professor.

1

Open the app and sit down

Open RayTok on my phone and pick my language. Phone on the desk; a seat near the front, where the professor is easy to hear, is best. I ask the professor for nothing.

2

Listen through earbuds, watch the professor

I hear my own language through open-ear RayTok earbuds, with my eyes on the professor and the board. Anything I miss, I check in the subtitles on my phone.

3

Notes remain after class

The lecture in the original and in translation stays on my phone. I reread what I missed and print it out as notes to revise from. Planned for the November 2026 release

Main features

From lectures to clubs, one set of my own

Lecture interpretation

My phone listens to the professor and turns it into my language. Even a lecture thick with technical terms comes through in my own words, and the on-screen subtitles let me check again.

Lecture notes · reports

When class ends, the original and the translation remain as notes, and a report sorted by date and course can be printed. For revising before exams. Planned for the November 2026 release

Conversations outside class

Hand over one earbud and talk face to face at a club, at a part-time job or with a friend. The other person installs nothing.

Microphone (optional)

In a large hall or a back-row seat, pair a small wireless microphone with my phone and place it up front. Ask the professor first and set it near the lectern.

Most languages work without internet

Download the language pack ahead of time and it runs without mobile data. A few languages need internet. No audio is stored — only the text remains.

Frequently asked questions

Check before you buy

Do I need permission from the professor or the university?

The app and earbuds are mine, so the university installs nothing. Since it does keep the lecture as text, follow your institution's rules on recording and note-taking, and tell the professor if you place a microphone on the lectern.

Does it work from the back of the room?

The clearer the professor's voice is from your seat, the better. In a large hall or a back row, we suggest placing a small wireless microphone up front.

Does my phone need internet?

For most languages, downloading the language pack in advance means no internet is needed. A few languages do need it.

Where do the lecture notes end up?

On my phone only. No audio is stored — just the text. Planned for the November 2026 release.

What if the professor lectures in English?

That's fine — just pick the language you want to hear.
