Frequently asked questions

Check before you start

Can workers listen by audio?

Yes. They listen through their own earphones. When listening in a browser, the screen must stay on for the audio to continue. At noisy sites, read the captions as well.

Can we use the company Wi-Fi?

Many Wi-Fi networks block device-to-device communication for security reasons. We recommend using a separate portable router with internet access (an LTE router or a wired connection). It must be a router that assigns addresses to connected devices itself, not a Wi-Fi extender (repeater).

What happens with languages that have no translation model on the phone?

When there is internet, the translation server translates into that language. Only when there is no internet is the text shown in another language the listener can read. For example, Nepali is shown in Hindi, Lao in Thai and Mongolian in Russian, and the screen says so, for example "No Nepali model; showing Hindi instead." For languages with no substitute, such as Uzbek, Khmer, Burmese and Sinhala, the screen says "This language requires internet."

Does it work with the instructor's phone hotspot?

For up to 3–5 people, the instructor's phone hotspot works. For more, a portable router with internet access is needed. When the hotspot is used, the workers' phones also use the instructor's mobile data line. While the instructor's phone is connected to a router's Wi-Fi, its hotspot cannot be turned on at the same time. If you use a router, the workers connect to the same router.

Can we not use it if the site has no internet?

Workers' phones do not need internet. For most languages, the instructor's phone also works without internet. The instructor's phone needs to reach LTE or Wi-Fi only when using some languages, such as Nepali and Burmese. We check this together at your site before you start.

What if the translation is wrong?

It is machine translation, so errors are possible. For important safety instructions, use demonstrations and visual materials as well. The original and the translation are shown together.

Is statutory training recognized on the basis of these records?

RayTok is an interpretation tool, not a safety and health training institution. Responsibility for conducting the training, documenting it and managing completion lies with the training institution and the employer. The records are material that supports that documentation.

Does the instructor need separate training?

Open the app and press Start. A 30-minute walkthrough is enough.
