RayTok Privacy Policy

Effective date 2026-09-04 · Applies to: RayTok Ear (com.raytok.ear), RayTok Glass (com.raytok.glass)

RayTok is a real-time translation app. RayTok does not operate servers. No content generated by the app is collected by the RayTok operator, and no analytics or advertising SDKs are used. This document details everything the app stores on your phone and transmits outside your phone without omission.

1. Information stored on your phone

DataLocationRetention periodHow to delete
Notes — Original text and translations from lectures, notes, and saved conversationsApp-dedicated storageUntil deleted by the userDelete notes, or Settings → Reset app
Activity log — Session begin and end linesApp-dedicated storage, recent 60 linesUntil supersededReset app
Settings — Languages, output, switches, translation engine, phone nameApp-dedicated storageUntil deletedReset app
Recognition log — Original text, timestamps, and settings provided by speech recognizer (for diagnostics)Memory; saved to Notes when the user taps "Save recognizer log to notes"Discarded when app closes; same as notes if savedClear logs, Reset app
Translation models (approx. 30–80MB per language)App-dedicated filesUntil deletedSettings → Language packs → Delete

Speech recognition models are managed by Google Speech Services on the phone and reside outside the app. Android Auto Backup is turned off in test builds. If enabled in release builds, the above items are backed up to the user's own Google account and can only be restored to the same account.

2. Information transmitted outside your phone

DataDestinationWhen transmitted
Sentences to translate (source text)Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com)Depending on translation engine settings — default is settled sentences only, "On-device first" transmits only languages without on-device models, "Always online" transmits all
Voice (audio)Google Speech Services on the phone — processed on-device if an on-device model exists, otherwise transmitted to Google serversWhen listening to a language without an on-device recognition model
Conversation lines (original + translation)Other phone connected via Two-phone — same local network, unencryptedDuring an active Two-phone connection

Sentences and voice audio transmitted to Google are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. To use the app without internet, download languages in advance in Settings → Language packs and set the translation engine to "On-device first". For Two-phone, a hotspot hosted by one phone is recommended over public Wi-Fi.

3. App permissions

  • Microphone — Speech recognition
  • Bluetooth connection — Transmitting audio to earbuds and glasses
  • Internet — Translation, downloading language packs
  • Camera — Scanning Two-phone QR codes (only while scanning)

4. User choices and controls

  • Settings → Reset app: Deletes all items in Section 1 (including recognition logs).
  • Delete individual notes on the Notes screen; delete models on the Language packs screen.
  • Before transferring or disposing of your phone, resetting the app or uninstalling the app is recommended.

5. Children and sensitive information

RayTok is not directed to children and does not collect separate account, registration, or payment information. Translation content is exactly as spoken by the user, and the app operator cannot access or view it.
