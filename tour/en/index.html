Frequently asked questions

Check before you start

Can guests keep listening by audio?

Yes. They listen through their own earphones. When listening in a browser, the screen must stay on for the audio to continue. Captions appear as well, so anything missed can be checked on the screen.

What happens with languages that have no translation model on the phone?

When listening by QR, if the phone does not carry that language, the server translates into that language directly (internet needed). When listening in the app, translation happens only inside the phone, so a language with no on-phone model is shown in another language the listener can read. For example, Nepali is shown in Hindi, Lao in Thai and Mongolian in Russian, and the screen says so, for example "No Nepali model; showing Hindi instead." For languages with no substitute, such as Uzbek, Khmer, Burmese and Sinhala, please listen by QR.

Does it work with the guide's phone hotspot?

For up to 3–5 people, the guide's phone hotspot works. For more, a portable router with internet access is needed. When the hotspot is used, the guests' phones also use the guide's mobile data line. While the guide's phone is connected to a router's Wi-Fi, its hotspot cannot be turned on at the same time. If you use a router, the guests connect to the same router.

What if a guest has no earphones?

They can read the captions on the screen. It helps if the travel agency keeps spare earphones ready.

Will the guide's phone battery hold up?

For long tours we recommend a power bank. Check it first on your actual route with the free trial.

What if the translation is wrong?

It is machine translation, so errors are possible. For announcements such as meeting times or hazardous areas, it helps to speak briefly and clearly.

What happens to guests' personal information?

Guests do not sign up. We do not collect contact details and we do not store audio.
